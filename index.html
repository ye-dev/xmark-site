XMark

Support

XMark is an iPhone audio recorder built around one idea: mark first, describe later. Tap Mark at the moments that matter while you record, then jump straight back to them.

Privacy policy

Questions, ideas or a bug? Use Support & feedback in the app’s Settings (the gear button next to “Recordings”), or email xyesinteractive@gmail.com.

For bug reports, it helps to include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your iPhone. XMark has no account and never uploads your recordings, markers or notes. They’re included in your normal iPhone or iCloud backup, like other app data.

How do I mark a moment?

While recording, tap the big Mark button. Each tap adds a marker at that point in the audio, with no typing needed. You can add a note to any marker later.

Can I mark from the Lock Screen?

Yes. While you record, a Live Activity on the Lock Screen has its own Mark button. If your iPhone isn’t unlocked by Face ID at that moment (for example, it’s lying face-up on a table), iOS asks for your passcode before the mark is added. That’s an iOS rule for Lock Screen buttons.

What happens if a call or another app interrupts my recording?

Recording pauses and everything captured so far is kept. It never resumes on its own; tap Resume when you’re ready.

What if my iPhone runs out of storage?

XMark won’t start a new recording when space is too low, and if storage runs critically low during a recording it stops and saves what was captured.

How do I export or share a recording?

Open the recording, tap the ••• menu and choose Export. You can share the audio alone (.m4a), the audio together with your markers, notes, summary and transcript, or just the text.

How do transcripts and summaries work?

After you stop a recording, XMark makes a transcript on your iPhone; nothing is uploaded. Tap any sentence in the Transcript tab to play from there, and each marker shows what was being said. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro and later), XMark also writes a summary with key points, action items and your marked moments. Transcripts need iOS 26 or later. You can turn both off, or pick the transcription language, in Settings.

Do tips unlock anything?

No. Tips are optional and every feature stays free for everyone. Purchases are handled by Apple; I never see your payment details.
