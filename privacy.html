XMark

Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Support

In short: your recordings, markers and notes stay on your iPhone. XMark has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only information that ever reaches me is what you choose to send through Support and feedback.

Who I am

XMark is made by Ye Liang, an independent developer. Questions about this policy: xyesinteractive@gmail.com.

Recordings, markers and notes

  • The microphone is used only while you are recording, after you give permission.
  • Recordings, markers and notes are stored only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded by the app.
  • They are included in your iPhone or iCloud backup, the same as other app data, under your Apple backup settings.
  • When you export, the file goes only where you send it through the iOS share sheet.
  • While you record, the Lock Screen Live Activity shows the recording timer and how many markers you’ve added.

Support and feedback

If you choose to send a message from the app, it includes:

  • the topic (question, idea or bug) and your message;
  • your email address, only if you enter one, so I can reply;
  • device information (app version, iOS version and iPhone model), unless you remove it before sending;
  • an app log, only if you leave “Include app log” on: the last 7 days of app events such as when a recording started, paused or stopped, when markers were added and whether exports succeeded. It contains times and durations only, never recording titles, notes or audio. You can preview it before sending.

Messages are stored in Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) in a database only I can read. Apple attaches an anonymous identifier for your iCloud account to each message; it does not reveal your name or Apple ID to me. If you use the email option instead, your message goes through your own email provider.

I use these messages only to answer you and to fix and improve XMark. I don’t sell or share them. I keep them for as long as they’re useful for that, and you can ask me to delete yours at any time by emailing me.

Tips

Tips are in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple. I receive no payment or card details, only Apple’s standard sales reports.

Children

XMark isn’t directed at children and doesn’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent a message with personal information, email me and I’ll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
